UT AI Safety Symposium

Monday, April 26, 2027 · Location TBD

The course ends with a student research conference built around the projects written in class. Attendance is required.

How it works

  • Project: Teams of up to 3 students submit an original AI safety project of up to 5 pages in NeurIPS format, along with an anonymized code repository (required).
  • Peer review: Projects are blind-reviewed by classmates on OpenReview.
  • Rebuttal: Authors respond to their reviews during a one-week discussion period, then may revise for the camera-ready version.
  • Poster: Every team presents a poster at the UT AI Safety Symposium.
  • 🏆 Oral presentation: The top-ranked projects are selected for an oral presentation.
